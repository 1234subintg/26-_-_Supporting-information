Selectivity control
mechanism.

A finely tuned adsorption-energy hierarchy allows PhBr to regulate access to the Pd surface while favoring release of the cis-alkene product.

Adsorption strength on Pd
Alkyne>PhBr>cis-Alkene

Alkyne binds most strongly, PhBr provides intermediate surface occupation, and the alkene product binds more weakly. This ordering promotes product desorption and suppresses overhydrogenation, thereby controlling semihydrogenation selectivity.
